M2 recall eligibility and recall/freeze serialization:
  Change: pinning locks the exact claim_revision FOR SHARE before looking for a matching RecallEvent. Freeze/release locks all pinned revisions FOR SHARE in claim_revision_id order and checks matching RecallEvents; matching uses claim_id plus NULL-or-equal revision id and content hash.
  Change: RecallEvent insertion locks matching revisions FOR NO KEY UPDATE in stable claim_revision_id order before quarantine scanning. This conflicts with FOR SHARE. All release_lot, membership, claim status/recall, and RecallEvent mutations acquire the same transaction advisory gate before tuple locks, avoiding the release_lot/revision lock-order inversion. Operations fail closed outside READ COMMITTED so an old repeatable-read snapshot cannot approve after waiting.
  Tests: RL-REC-1 exact and claim-wide recalled pin attempts; RL-REC-2 recall after pinning blocks freeze while status remains APPROVED. tests/run_release_lot_concurrency.sh proves blocker/waiter relationships via pg_blocking_pids and pg_locks for approval withdrawal first/freeze first (RL-CONC-A/B), both RecallEvent/freeze orderings (RL-CONC-C/D), and an actual revision FOR SHARE blocker against the RecallEvent FOR NO KEY UPDATE lock (RL-CONC-E). Tests assert SQLSTATE P0001 and exact error messages.
